隐私政策

街角讲解员（CornerStories） · 开发者：WEIWEI SONG · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 11 日

街角讲解员是一款伦敦城市导览应用：内置 96 个伦敦地点故事，覆盖 9 个片区，可按当前位置显示附近故事，并提供中文语音讲解、亲子讲解模式、地图、搜索、收藏、听过记录与散步路线提醒。

我们收集什么数据

我们不收集任何数据。本应用没有账号、没有登录、没有广告、没有第三方 SDK、没有数据分析、没有后端服务器，也不会将任何信息上传到任何服务器。

位置信息

如果你允许使用位置，位置仅在你的 iPhone 本机使用，用于在地图上显示附近的历史地点、按距离排序故事，以及在你主动开启散步模式时提示附近故事。位置不会上传、不会存储到服务器、不会与任何第三方共享。你可以拒绝定位权限，拒绝后仍可按片区浏览全部故事。

语音讲解

语音讲解使用 iOS 系统自带的语音合成（AVSpeechSynthesizer）在设备上播放，不联网、不上传录音，本应用不使用麦克风。

本机保存的内容

你的收藏、听过记录和设置只保存在你自己的设备上（App 的本地存储），我们无法看到，也不会同步到任何地方。删除应用后，这些本地记录会随之删除。

儿童

亲子讲解模式只是另一种讲解风格。本应用不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童的数据，也没有面向儿童的账号或聊天功能。

内容来源

每个地点故事都附有资料来源链接。部分故事标注为“初稿”，表示根据公开资料撰写、尚未逐条人工核对来源，年份和细节请以来源链接的官方页面为准。

联系我们

如对本隐私政策或应用有任何问题，请联系：cornerstories.support@gmail.com。

政策更新

如本政策有更新，我们会在本页面更新生效日期。应用本身不收集数据，因此政策更新不会改变任何数据处理方式。

Privacy Policy

CornerStories (街角讲解员) · Developer: WEIWEI SONG · Effective: 11 November 2026

CornerStories is a London city guide app with 96 built-in stories about places in London across 9 areas. It shows nearby stories based on your location and offers Chinese audio narration, a child-friendly narration mode, maps, search, favourites, listening history and walking-route reminders.

What data we collect

We collect no data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no third-party SDKs, no analytics and no backend server, and nothing is uploaded to any server.

Location

If you grant location permission, your location is used only on your iPhone to show nearby historic places on the map, sort stories by distance, and alert you to nearby stories when you start a walking reminder. Location is never uploaded, never stored on a server and never shared with any third party. You can deny location permission and still browse all stories by area.

Audio narration

Narration uses the built-in iOS speech synthesiser (AVSpeechSynthesizer) on your device. It does not need the internet and does not upload recordings. The app does not use the microphone.

What is stored on your device

Your favourites, listening history and settings are stored only on your own device (the app's local storage). We cannot see them and they are not synced anywhere. Deleting the app deletes these local records.

Children

The child-friendly mode is only a different narration style. The app collects no data from anyone, including children, and has no accounts or chat features for children.

Sources

Each story links to its source. Some stories are marked as drafts, meaning they were written from public sources and have not yet been individually checked against those sources; please rely on the linked official pages for dates and details.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or the app, contact us at cornerstories.support@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page. Because the app collects no data, a policy change does not change how any data is handled.
